ABTA (own application), ATOL if triggered, ORN Nexus PTE shareholding & funding, UK bank account, share structure, VAT. Gates supplier credit, bonding and clean invoicing.

Gates: Lanes 1·2·3·7

One master set of passenger volumes, segment mix (Sports / Group Leisure / MICE / FIT) and accommodation spend. Feeds Addison Lee, IHG, cruise and product — build once, reuse everywhere.

Standard mutual NDA held ready. Required before detailed figures pass to Addison Lee, IHG and Sports Hotel. Own the template so it never blocks a deal.
